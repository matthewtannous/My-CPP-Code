Make sure args in tasks.json is like this when compiling many files at once

"args": [
                "-fdiagnostics-color=always",
                "-g",
                "${fileDirname}\\**.cpp",
                "-o",
                "${fileDirname}\\${fileBasenameNoExtension}.exe"
            ],

                "-Wconversion",
